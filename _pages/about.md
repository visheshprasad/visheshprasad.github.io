I am a current MS student in ECE with a specialization in Machine Learning at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I graduated in May 2025 from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign with a B.S. in Computer Engineering and minors in Mathematics, Statistics, and Econometrics.

My interests broadly lie in applied machine learning for real-world systems, with a particular focus on reinforcement learning, adversarial ML & robustness, generative modeling, multi-agent systems, game theory, computer vision, and robotics. I'm especially driven by solving real-world problems with machine learning, studying learning in complex environments, and optimizing existing processes to build systems that create meaningful impact.
